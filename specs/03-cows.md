# Cows

## Purpose

Let the farm owner record cows kept for milk or meat.

## Cow record

A cow record can include:

- Tag number
- Name, if known
- Breed, if known
- Date of birth, if known
- Purpose: milk, meat, or both
- Gender
- Color, if known
- Weight, if known
- Age, if known
- Acquired by: purchased, or born on farm
- Purchase date, if purchased and known
- Purchase price, if purchased and known
- Notes

## Milk production

For a cow kept for milk, the owner can record:

- Date and time of production
- Quantity of milk
- Unit of measurement
- Price per unit
- Optional note

## Feeding

For any cow, the owner can record:

- Date and time of feeding
- Feed type or name
- Quantity
- Unit of measurement
- Optional note

## Acceptance criteria

- The owner can add, view, edit, and archive a cow record.
- The owner can mark whether a cow was purchased or born on the farm.
- Purchase date and purchase price apply only when the cow was purchased.
- The owner can record milk production for a cow.
- The owner can review a cow’s milk production entries.
- A cow kept only for meat does not require milk production records.
- The owner can record feeding for a cow.
- The owner can review a cow’s feeding entries.
