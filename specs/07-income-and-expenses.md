# Income and Expenses

## Purpose

Let the farm owner record general farm income and expenses.

## Entry fields

Each entry includes:

- Type: income or expense
- Date
- Category
- Amount
- Unit of measurement
- Total price
- Optional note

Examples of income include livestock or product sales.

Examples of expenses include costs that are not already recorded against a crop cycle.

## Acceptance criteria

- The owner can add, view, edit, and archive an income or expense entry.
- Entries can be viewed by date.
- The owner can see simple total income and total expenses for a selected period.

Crop expenses should be recorded in the Crops module and should not be counted a second time as general expenses.
