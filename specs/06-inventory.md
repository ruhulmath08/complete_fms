# Inventory

## Purpose

Let the farm owner track supplies and update their quantities.

## Inventory item

Each item can include:

- Item name
- Category
- Quantity
- Unit of measurement
- Price per unit
- Total price
- Optional note

## Stock updates

The owner can record stock being added or used. Each update includes:

- Date
- Quantity added or used
- Reason or note

The displayed quantity should reflect the recorded stock updates.

## Acceptance criteria

- The owner can add, view, edit, and archive an inventory item.
- The owner can record stock being added or used.
- The owner can see the current quantity.
- The system prevents a stock update from reducing the quantity below zero unless the product owner later decides otherwise.
