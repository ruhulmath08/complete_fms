# Poultry

## Purpose

Let the farm owner track poultry in groups.

## Poultry group

Each group includes:

- Type: broiler, duck, layer, or local
- Group name or identifier
- Number of birds
- Date acquired, if known
- Acquired by: purchased, or born on farm
- Purchase date, if purchased and known
- Purchase price, if purchased and known
- Notes

The owner can update the number of birds when birds are added or removed.

## Feeding

For any poultry group, the owner can record:

- Date and time of feeding
- Feed type or name
- Quantity
- Unit of measurement
- Optional note

## Egg production

For a layer, duck, or local group, the owner can record:

- Date and time of production
- Quantity of eggs
- Unit of measurement
- Price per unit
- Optional note

A broiler group does not require egg production records.

## Acceptance criteria

- The owner can add, view, edit, and archive a poultry group.
- The owner can update its bird count.
- The owner can record feeding for a poultry group.
- The owner can review a poultry group’s feeding entries.
- The owner can record egg production for a layer, duck, or local group.
- The owner can review a poultry group’s egg production entries.
- A broiler group does not require egg production records.
