# Farm Management Project — Product Brief

## Purpose

Help one farm owner keep track of livestock, crops, supplies, income, and expenses.

## First-version scope

The first version supports one farm and one owner account. It provides basic record keeping for:

- Sign up and sign in
- Cow records for meat and milk, including milk production
- Poultry groups for broilers, ducks, layers, and local birds
- Crop cycles and harvest records
- Crop costs, including labour, fertilizers, pesticides and chemicals, seeds, irrigation, equipment, and other costs
- Inventory records and stock updates
- Simple income and expense entries
- Basic farm settings

## Users

The first version has one user: the farm owner. The owner can manage all farm information.

## Out of scope

The first version does not include:

- Multiple farms
- Multiple owner accounts or staff roles
- Advanced accounting
- Forecasting
- Complex reports
