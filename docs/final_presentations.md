# Final Presentations

This document contains the final presentations for the project and our E2E course. This presentations should contain the following:

```Email
AI-DLC End-to-End Course – Final Presentation on Sunday
 
The presentation should focus primarily on your AI-DLC journey, not just on the project you built. The project is the secondary output; your experience of applying the AI-DLC process is the primary output.
 
Please keep your presentation within 3–4 slides, following this guideline:
 
Slide 1 – Starting Point
 
Your professional role/background
 
The project/problem you selected
 
What you initially expected
 
Slide 2 – My AI-DLC Journey
 
How you followed the AI-DLC process
 
Key phases/gates you experienced
 
Important artifacts or outputs
 
How AI supported your work
 
You may refer to AWS AI-DLC or Specs.md AI-DLC phases such as:
Intent → Inception → Requirements → Design → Construction → Testing → Deployment
 
Slide 3 – Challenges & Discoveries
 
2–3 important challenges, iterations, or unexpected findings
 
What you initially wanted vs. what actually happened
 
Something AI-DLC changed, improved, or helped you discover
 
Slide 4 – Outcome & Learning
 
What you finally built
 
What you learned from applying AI-DLC
 
How your way of working changed
 
What you can now do differently
 
Key message:
Do not present only “what I built.” Present “how I was able to build it using AI-DLC, what I experienced, what I learned, and how the journey changed my way of working.”
 
You are free to use your own presentation style, screenshots, artifacts, diagrams, or demo. These slides are only a guideline to ensure that the AI-DLC journey remains the primary focus.
```
